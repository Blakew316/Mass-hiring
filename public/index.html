          Mac relay

          checking…
          Setting it up on the Mac — three steps
          1. Generate a relay token below and copy it.
          2. On the Mac: cd Mass-hiring/relay && ./install.sh, paste the token into the config it creates, then run it again.
          3. Grant the two macOS permissions it asks for: Automation, so it can tell Messages to send, and Full Disk Access, so it can read delivery receipts, read receipts and replies.

          Without Automation nothing sends; without Full Disk Access texts go out but nothing comes back. Full instructions are in relay/README.md.

          A separate secret from your dashboard password. It unlocks the relay routes and nothing else.

          Pace & hours

          Up to 100. Above that, Apple bans accounts.
          The wait is randomised between the two.
          A fixed interval is the clearest bot signal there is.
