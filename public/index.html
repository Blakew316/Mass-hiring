    Find candidates with Apollo

    not set up

    Searches Apollo's database and adds the people it finds straight to your list, with their current role, employer, location and previous roles. Searching is free; each email address Apollo reveals costs one of your Apollo credits.

    People one to two and a half years into a job answer recruiting mail most often. Anyone already on your list is skipped, and nobody from your own company is ever added.
    Get the key from Apollo → Settings → Integrations → API: press Connect, then copy the key it shows. It is saved with your other settings, masked afterwards, and never sent to the browser again. API access needs a paid Apollo plan; if yours does not have it, the key is refused and this card says so.

      Mac relay

      checking…

      Apple has no server-side iMessage API, so the sending is done by a small service on your Mac Studio that drives Messages directly — nothing extra to install. It calls out to this dashboard, so nothing has to reach into your Mac and there are no ports to open.

      1. Generate a relay token below and copy it.
      2. On the Mac: cd Mass-hiring/relay && ./install.sh, paste the token into the config it creates, then run it again.
      3. Grant the two macOS permissions it asks for: Automation, so it can tell Messages to send, and Full Disk Access, so it can read delivery receipts, read receipts and replies.

      Without Automation nothing sends; without Full Disk Access texts go out but nothing comes back. Full instructions are in relay/README.md.

      A separate secret from your dashboard password. It unlocks the relay routes and nothing else.

      Pace & hours

      Apple disables iMessage on accounts that blast strangers, so this is capped well below where that starts. Texts also only go out during daytime hours where the person lives, which is both politer and what the TCPA requires.

      Up to 100. Above that, Apple bans accounts.
      The wait is randomised between the two.
      A fixed interval is the clearest bot signal there is.

      Calendly

      Bookings flip the candidate to “Booked” and push to your phone; your upcoming interviews sync into the Interviews booked tile every few minutes.

      Sending pace

      Large sends go out automatically in the background at this pace. Google Workspace allows about 2,000 messages per account per rolling 24 hours (500 on free Gmail) and the Gmail API accepts about 60 sends a minute — those are Google's ceilings, so higher numbers are capped to them. The defaults leave headroom for your normal email.

      Up to 2,000 (Google Workspace). When it is reached the queue pauses and resumes by itself as the 24-hour window frees up.
      Up to 60 (Gmail's API quota). Several emails are sent in parallel each minute to keep this pace.

      People who were emailed and never replied become due for a follow-up after the wait; the Follow up buttons show how many are due.

      Apollo (candidate search)

      Lets the Import page pull new candidates from Apollo. Get a key from Apollo → Settings → Integrations → API (press Connect, then copy it) and paste it here, then press Save settings at the top of this page.

      Searching costs nothing. Revealing an email costs one Apollo credit, and the Import page always shows how many a batch will use before you confirm.
