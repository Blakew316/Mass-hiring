    Key terms in plain language

    A summary for your convenience — the agreement itself governs.

    • Independent contractor. You are an independent contractor, not an employee. You cover your own expenses and are not eligible for employee benefits (§8.5, §2.1).
    • Term. Three years to start, renewing yearly after that (§6.1).
    • Exclusivity. You work merchant services exclusively for Wholesale Payments. Breach carries liquidated damages of the greater of the account's value or $1,500 per breach (§1.7(B)).
    • Non-solicitation. For five years after you leave, you may not solicit WP merchants. Damages are the greater of $1,500 or 40× the merchant's monthly value (§5.1, §5.3).
    • Commissions & residuals. Set by Schedule A, which WP may change. Residuals stop below $250/month unless vested — three consecutive months at $5,000+ and 30 processing merchants (§2.3).
    • Chargebacks. Commissions can be charged back for up to 120 days; equipment for 45 days.
    • Authorizations. Signing authorizes WP to run a consumer credit report and to credit and debit your bank account (§3.3, §2.3(C)).
    • Changes. WP may amend the agreement on three days' notice (§8.8).
    • Disputes. The agreement includes a jury trial and class action waiver (§8.15).
