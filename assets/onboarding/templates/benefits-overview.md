# Benefits Overview

Welcome, {{firstName}}! Here's a summary of the benefits available to you as a {{employmentType}} employee at {{companyName}}. Full plan documents are available in BambooHR under **Files → Benefits**.

## Health & Wellness

- **Medical** — Multiple plan tiers; coverage begins the first of the month after your start date
- **Dental & Vision** — Company-sponsored plans for you and your dependents
- **Mental health** — Employer-paid access to counseling and wellness sessions

## Financial

- **401(k)** — Eligible from day one, with company matching
- **Life & disability insurance** — Employer-paid basic coverage, optional supplemental
- **FSA/HSA** — Pre-tax accounts depending on your medical plan election

## Time Off

- **PTO** — Flexible paid time off, coordinated with your manager
- **Holidays** — Published company holiday calendar
- **Parental leave** — Paid leave for new parents

## How to Enroll

1. Log in to BambooHR with the credentials sent to your inbox
2. Go to **Benefits** and review your plan options
3. Make your elections within **30 days** of your start date
4. Questions? Contact {{hrContactName}} at {{hrContactEmail}}

> Enrollment deadline matters: if you miss the 30-day window, you may need to wait for open enrollment to make changes.
