# Payroll & Required Forms

Hi {{firstName}} — a few required forms need your attention before or on your first day ({{startDate}}). Here's the short list and how to complete each one.

## Form I-9 — Employment Eligibility Verification

- **Section 1** must be completed by you no later than your first day
- Bring acceptable identity/work-authorization documents on day one (e.g., a passport, or a driver's license plus Social Security card — the full list is on the form)
- The form and instructions: uscis.gov/i-9

## Form W-4 — Federal Tax Withholding

- Determines your federal income tax withholding
- Complete it in BambooHR during onboarding, or download it at irs.gov/w4
- You can update your withholding at any time

## Direct Deposit

- Set up in BambooHR under **Pay Info → Direct Deposit**
- Have your bank routing and account numbers handy
- Changes made by the payroll cutoff apply to your next paycheck

## State Forms

- Depending on your work location ({{workLocation}}), a state tax withholding form may also be required — {{hrContactName}} will confirm which apply to you
